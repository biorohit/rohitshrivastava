Fellowship applications / 2019–2020

Applying for Alexander von Humboldt (AvH) postdoctoral fellowship

By Rohit Shrivastava · 5 min read

In May 2019, during search for postdoc position I sent my request email to a Professor in Wurzburg, Germany. Although I never had a chance to meet her in person but always followed and appreciated her work. When I applied to work with her, I had no idea what she was currently working on, however, he pioneering work motivated me to work with her.

Reply to my request email was a mix of disappointment and hope. Disappointment as she has no means to fund a postdoc and hope because she suggested that I can apply for a fellowship to work in her lab. After an exchange of few email we decided that I spent a week in her lab to get to know the ongoing work, formulate a research idea and the available research infrastructure. I agreed and after obtaining a German Visa, took a flight to Frankfurt. During interactions with her I got to know that she was also one of the reviewer for my paper. During the 5 days I got to know members of her lab and had a good interaction about the what we can achieve in the available infrastructure. Also I presented my work to the department and got to know the researchers. Although we scavenged part of her ERC grant data but by the end of my stay I had the plan for fellowship in my mind.

Coming back to Israel, I tried to read and write about things which I have never written about in past. Writing to justify why you are the best candidate to get this fellowship or how getting this fellowship will help develop the career in future was really difficult as we tend to undervalue ourselves. However, it was a good exercise. We submitted in August and waited to hear the results.

In March 2020 the results came and I was a disappointed with the Humboldt foundation as they 7 took months to decide and did not let me update my publications that got accepted during the evaluation period. After a wait of 7 months, Humboldt foundation notified me that my application was not selected for fellowship, reason being I don’t have enough publications and that I cannot reapply for 18 months. It was really disheartening to be rejected because of silly policy. I felt my prospective PI was more disappointed with the decision, as she was really hopeful that I will get it.

Although I couldn’t work with her but the experience I gained of writing fellowship will surely be with me for long time and will help in future applications.

How to apply for Humboldt Research Fellowship for postdoctoral researchers?

Although instructions from the Humboldt foundation are self explanatory but I have included few pointers to apply.

  1. Application had to be filled and submitted online.
  2. Besides general information, professional details, details of current institute, address for correspondence, details of host and host institution are required.
  3. In addition, two documents need to be uploaded by the host PI – host statement & hosts confirmation of research facilities.
  4. Host statement includes an expert opinion from the host about the applicant’s academic record, achievements, publications and research proposal submitted by the applicant.
  5. Host confirmation of research facilities include a statement by the PI confirming that the host institute has the required infrastructure to successfully implement the agreed research work.
  6. Next includes description of academic and professional experience.
  7. Next includes field of research for the submitted application, details of doctoral thesis and doctoral degree.
  8. Now comes the main stuff – Title of the proposed work (200 characters), followed by keywords that best describe the work and a brief summary of 2000 characters to describe the research proposal.
  9. Next, in 2000 characters we need to decribe how the proposed research will impact the further development of own academic profile. In summary one needs to describe here how undertaking this research project will lead to development of the career of the applicant. One can connect the research experience they will gain during the fellowship, supervision of masters/PhD students, teaching that they will do, conferences they will attend, international mobility, publications and financial and administrative management of their own fellowship. All these aspects have to be related to development of their career as a mature scientist.
  10. Next are general information about languages the applicant knows, if they know german? if they have previously applied to Humboldt fellowship? if the applicant is applying to any other fellowship.
  11. Next one need to list the names and contact details of expert reviewers who will provide letters of support for the applicant. They will need to upload their letters directly to the link provided.
  12. Lastly, additional documents are required such as 2 page CV, complete list of publications, if any manuscript is under review or submitted then the letter of publisher needs to be uploaded, key publications needs to be highlighted with a paragraph detailing why the work done is important and your contribution, either doctoral certificate or a letter from supervisor indicating when thesis will be submitted and a 5 pages research outline (very important).

The research outline

Research Outline is the most critical document for the application. It should include an introduction, Aims, Objectives, Methodology, Experimental details, Significance of the proposal, Future prospects of the research, Gantt chart, Selection of the host and experience of the applicant. Each page of the document should carry the name of the applicant and programme of humoldt foundation for which application is submitted.
