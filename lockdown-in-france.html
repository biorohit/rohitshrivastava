Research life / Personal reflections

Lockdown in France

By Rohit Shrivastava · 2 min read

After a month and half of moving to Montpellier, to control the spread of coronavirus France entered a general lockdown. My institute was closed indefinitely and we were asked to complete or trash the experiments on 17th March and stay at home until further notice. Initially I liked it because i could catch up with all the web-series that I missed. But after a week of binge watch and being locked in home it started to get depressing. I lost track of time and was sleeping late night and waking up late.

By now news media was full of conspiracy theories and fake news. In this period, i got time to connect with my extended family and distant cousins.

Gradually we started to have a weekly lab meeting to discuss things and I started to write a research proposal. I also got time to attend lab meetings of my PhD lab and attend concluding seminars of previous lab mates.

Overall, France was locked down for approx 2 months, during which I managed to formulate a research plan for fellowships with the help of my PI. Being all alone in a new country without speaking the local language, I don’t really know how did I survive two months of lockdown.

“Sometimes it is best to just keep doing our job and not to think about the things which are not in our control”
